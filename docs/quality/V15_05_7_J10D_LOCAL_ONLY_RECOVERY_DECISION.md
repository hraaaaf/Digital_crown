# V1.5-05.7-J10D — LOCAL-ONLY recovery security decision (owner direction; design-only)

Date: 2026-10-20. Scope: LOT05 / 05.7-J10D only. Baseline: `eadd1880bdd5d5b8f6e1d6554ca1f47138120d63`.
Status: **LOCAL-ONLY PRODUCT DIRECTION ACCEPTED; J10D SECURITY GATE STILL OPEN / NO-GO**. This document supersedes the *recommendation* of a hosted witness in `V15_05_7_J10D_INDEPENDENT_WITNESS_AUTHORITY_CANDIDATE.md`, but does not erase that prior research, its observed counterexample, or the existing required-gate evidence. No server, account, hardware purchase, subscription, clinical runtime modification, or deployment is authorized.

## Decision and strict limits
Digital Crown remains local-first and fully usable for ordinary clinical work without Internet. Do not provision or require a cloud witness or second computer. Prefer existing local Windows facilities and established backup/restore paths rather than inventing a new infrastructure component. This decision **does not** certify J10D or silently downgrade previously approved security requirements: any conflict between this reduced threat model and the existing independent-authority acceptance gate is an explicit human product-security waiver/requirements change to resolve before any GO.

## Minimal local recovery contract (proposed; not implemented)
1. **Authenticated backups**: consistent snapshot of encrypted SQLCipher DB with WAL/SHM handling, media/vault and required metadata; a canonical, versioned manifest containing component hashes, backup identity, source installation and recovery prerequisites. Authenticate the manifest using a key whose custody, exportability and recovery behavior are documented; encryption alone does not prove authenticity. Reject corrupt, partial, mismatched or unverifiable sets. Preserve legitimate disaster recovery when the original machine is lost: do not bind recoverability exclusively to an irreplaceable TPM key.
2. **Controlled restore**: privileged authenticated operator, explicit informed confirmation of timestamp and potential data loss, independent preflight of complete backup, restore into a separate staging location, validated atomic/safe activation with rollback plan, and pre-restore safety copy when possible. On failed validation, incomplete media, unsupported schema, interrupted restore or ambiguous outcome: **DENY and preserve original state**, never silently bootstrap or auto-approve. Log an audit record without patient secrets.
3. **Windows durability**: least-privileged SCM service and strict NTFS ACL/reparse/symlink/swap checks for DB, WAL/SHM, backups and recovery staging; close handles and enforce tested write/flush/rename ordering. Test process kill, Windows reboot and **physical power cut** on dedicated synthetic-data hardware; verify no silent clinical data loss or incoherent mixed-version recovery. Do not treat a successful `Flush(true)` simulation as physical power-loss proof.
4. **Threat boundary**: protect against accidental restore, ordinary user abuse, corrupted/tampered backups, partial write, unauthorized application-level restore and common local file manipulation. **Not guaranteed**: detection of malicious full-volume rollback by an administrator with control of all local files, signing keys and snapshots; no independent monotonic authority exists. A local TPM-backed key alone does not fix this.
5. **Operational offline behavior**: normal use and local recovery need no Internet. Any unrecoverable authenticity/key-custody conflict must stop and require documented manual incident handling; no fail-open. Backup export/rotation, retention, recovery credentials, lost-PC migration and separation from live clinical data need explicit design review.

## Acceptance and gate classification
- **REQUIRED before local-recovery release**: executable negative tests for manifest integrity, wrong/missing key, mixed DB/WAL/media, unauthorized operator, partial/corrupt backup, aborted restore and preservation of original state; genuine Windows SCM/ACL probes; physical power-cut and full restore drills with synthetic data; evidence on exact reviewed SHA, plus independent adversarial reviews and explicit product-security acceptance of reduced threat model.
- **EXPERIMENTAL, never product proof**: existing J10D same-host FAKE Ed25519/SQLite witness and 45-test Windows/Linux lab. Its full rollback reissue is an observed counterexample, not a fixed defect.
- **REQUIRED under original anti-rollback objective, currently UNSATISFIED**: independently maintained monotonic state and trust custody. No hosted authority is authorized. Therefore original J10D independent-authority gate **remains NO-GO**, unless the owner formally approves a scoped requirements change with residual-risk acceptance.
- **HORS SCOPE**: production implementation, real patient restores, cloud/vendor procurement, other lots, merging, deployment.

## Adversarial design review (internal only, NOT independent)

B — reliability, severe 3/10 for product readiness: no executed end-to-end restore or physical Windows power-loss experiment; DB/WAL/media atomicity and lost-key recovery not proven. BLOCKER.
