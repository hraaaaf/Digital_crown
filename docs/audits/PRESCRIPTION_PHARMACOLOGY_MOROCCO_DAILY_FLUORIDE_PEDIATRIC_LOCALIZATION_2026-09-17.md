## Goal

## Morocco evidence

- SensiVital toothpaste; official page states fluoride 1450 ppm and age 7+.
- GUM Kids and Junior toothpaste ranges are listed on the Morocco children page.
- GUM Morocco professional brochure states product-specific concentrations: Kids 3+ at 500 ppm and Junior 6+ at 1450 ppm.
- GUM Morocco lists Baby 0-2, Kids 2-6, Junior 6-9 and Teens 10+ toothbrush families.
- GUM Junior mouthrinse is listed locally; this confirms market presence only and does not create a generic pediatric mouthrinse recommendation.

## Clinical guard

The canonical daily-fluoride family remains age/product/guideline specific. A Morocco product example with a stated fluoride concentration is sufficient for market localization, not for universal dosing or recommendation.

Pediatric product labels and contemporary clinical guidance must be reconciled before any patient-facing activation; older brand brochure concentrations must not be generalized to all current products or to all children.
